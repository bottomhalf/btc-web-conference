Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to BTC Web Conference. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected through our web-based video conferencing application, which utilizes LiveKit technology, chat services, and local data indexing via Dexie.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the application, express an interest in obtaining information about us, or otherwise when you contact us.

  • Account Details: Name, email address, password, and profile picture.
  • Audio and Video Data: During meetings, we process real-time audio and video streams. We do not record or store these streams unless explicitly requested by the host and indicated to all participants.
  • Communication Data: Chat messages exchanged during a conference.
  • Device and Usage Data: IP address, browser characteristics, operating system, and information on how you use our platform.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect or receive:

  • To facilitate account creation and logon process.
  • To enable real-time WebRTC communications (video, audio, screen sharing).
  • To manage user accounts and provide customer support.
  • To enforce our terms, conditions, and policies for business purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

We use local storage (via IndexedDB and Dexie) to cache certain meeting data and preferences for performance improvements. We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, despite our safeguards and efforts to secure your information, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

5. Your Privacy Rights

In some regions (like the EEA, UK, and California), you have certain rights under applicable data protection laws. These may include the right (i) to request access and obtain a copy of your personal information, (ii) to request rectification or erasure; (iii) to restrict the processing of your personal information; and (iv) if applicable, to data portability. To make such a request, please use the Help Center or our Account Deletion page.
